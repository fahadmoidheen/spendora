Privacy

Last updated 18 November 2026

Spendora is a personal expense tracker. This page explains in plain words what it stores about you, where it’s kept, and how to remove it.

What Spendora stores

  • Your entries – for each expense or income: the amount, date, category, title and note you give it. Also the monthly budgets you set.
  • An account ID – a code from the login service (for example google-oauth2|1048…) that ties your entries to your account.

That’s all. Spendora’s database doesn’t hold your name, email address, photo or password.

Your login

Logging in is handled by Auth0 (part of Okta). Auth0 keeps your email address, your name and profile photo, and – if you log in with email – your password, which Spendora never sees. If you choose “Continue with Google”, Google tells Auth0 who you are; Spendora gets no access to your Google account. The app shows your name, email and photo from your login; it doesn’t copy them anywhere.

Where your data lives

Spendora runs on these services. Each one handles data only to run its part of the app.

Neon The database with your entries and budgets. (Neon is part of Databricks.)
Render Runs the server that saves and loads your entries.
Netlify Delivers the app’s files to your browser.
Auth0 Login and your account details.
Google “Continue with Google”, if you use it. The app’s fonts also come from Google Fonts, so Google sees your IP address when they download.

Like any website, these services record technical details such as IP addresses in their logs, for security and to keep things running.

On your device

  • Your browser keeps your login (so you stay logged in), your currency and light/dark choice, and which tips you’ve dismissed. The installed app also keeps its own files so it opens offline. Logging out ends the login; clearing the site’s data removes the rest.
  • CSV imports and PDF reports are made in your browser. An imported file isn’t uploaded – only the rows you choose to import are saved.

What Spendora doesn’t do

  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking.
  • Your data is never sold or shared for anyone else’s use.
  • No other Spendora user can see your entries.

Keeping it safe

Everything travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Every request to the server carries a short-lived login token, which the server checks, and every database query is limited to your own account.

Your choices

  • See, change or delete any entry or budget in the app, at any time.
  • Download all your entries as a CSV file from the Transactions panel.
  • To delete your account and everything in it, email privacy@example.com. Your entries and budgets are deleted from the database and your Auth0 account is removed. The database provider’s short restore history may hold a copy for a few days more, until it expires.
